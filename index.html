Top 5 Hotels with EV Charging & Food Guide
自驾新山必看！自带充电桩 + 周边吃喝攻略

1. Amari Johor Bahru

阿玛瑞酒店 (City Centre) 📍 Opposite CIQ⚡ Limited Slots
🔋 Charging Info (充电情报): JomCharge (AC) at Level 6 Parking. Paid. 位于6楼停车场。位子较少，建议避开高峰。
🍽️ Eat & Play (吃喝玩乐): Walk to City Square, JBCC & Pasar Karat Night Market. Must try: Hiap Joo Banana Cake. 步行可达 City Square, 陈旭年街 (必买 Hiap Joo 香蕉蛋糕), 和 Karat 夜市。

2. St. Giles Southkey

圣吉尔斯酒店 (Mid Valley) 🛍️ Shopping Mall⚡ Many Chargers
🔋 Charging Info (充电情报): Tesla Supercharger + Gentari DC/AC at Mall parking. 楼下商场停车场有大量充电桩，全柔佛最多。
🍽️ Eat & Play (吃喝玩乐): Direct access to Mid Valley Southkey Mall. Din Tai Fung, Oriental Kopi & Cinema. 直通 Mid Valley 商场。推荐：鼎泰丰、东方咖啡、逛街看电影超方便。

3. Hard Rock Hotel Desaru

迪沙鲁硬石酒店 (Beach) 🌊 Family Trip⚡ Public Hub
🔋 Charging Info (充电情报): Tesla V3 + ChargEV at Desaru Public Parking nearby. 主要使用旁边的公共充电站，旺季需排队。
🎡 Eat & Play (吃喝玩乐): Next to Adventure Waterpark. 10min drive to Sg. Rengit for Lobster seafood. 就在水上乐园旁边。开车10分钟去四湾岛 (Sg Rengit) 吃龙虾海鲜。

4. Sunway Hotel Big Box

双威大盒子酒店 (Legoland) 🎢 Tuas Link⚡ Easy Access
🔋 Charging Info (充电情报): Tesla Supercharger + Gentari DC right at the mall. 靠近大士关口。商场有超充，通常有位。
🎡 Eat & Play (吃喝玩乐): Walking distance to X Park. 5min drive to Legoland Malaysia. BookXcess (huge bookstore). 步行可达 X Park 极限运动。开车5分钟到 Legoland。楼下有超美 BookXcess 书店。

5. Impiana Hotel Senai

士乃机场酒店 (Airport) ✈️ Business⚠️ Slow Charge Only
🔋 Charging Info (充电情报): 3-pin socket / Slow AC only. Use fast charger nearby. 只有慢充。建议先在附近油站快充后再入住。
🛍️ Eat & Play (吃喝玩乐): 10min drive to Johor Premium Outlets (JPO). Famous Senai delicious food nearby. 开车10分钟到 JPO 名牌城。附近有很多士乃地道美食。
