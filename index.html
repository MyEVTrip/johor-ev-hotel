柔佛电动车自驾游住宿指南 (2026 Edition)

Driving a Tesla, BYD or Proton e.MAS to Johor? Here are the top 5 hotels where you can sleep & charge.
开电动车来柔佛玩？这 5 家酒店让你睡觉时也能满电复活。

1. Amari Johor Bahru JB City Centre

Why here: Unbeatable location directly opposite CIQ & City Square Mall. Perfect for short trips. 推荐理由： 位置无敌，就在关口 CIQ 对面。非常适合刚通关不想跑远的车主。

🔋 EV Status (充电情报): Paid 付费 Limited Slots 位少
  • Charger: 2x JomCharge AC (Level 6 Parking).
  • Note: Slots are limited. Avoid peak hours.
    位子有限 (仅2-3个)，建议避开周末高峰。

2. St. Giles Southkey Mid Valley Mall

Why here: Located right above the biggest mall in JB (Mid Valley). Best charging hub in town. 推荐理由： 楼下就是 Mid Valley 商场，充电选择全柔佛最多！

🔋 EV Status (充电情报): Paid 付费 Many Slots 位多
  • Charger: 8x Tesla Superchargers + Gentari DC/AC.
  • Note: Very safe & convenient. Charge while you shop.
    位子非常充足，就在商场停车场，可以边逛街边充。
Check Prices & Book ➔ 查看房价 & 预订

3. Hard Rock Hotel Desaru Desaru Coast

Why here: Best for family vacation & waterpark access. Walking distance to public DC chargers. 推荐理由： 周末家庭度假首选。虽然酒店内桩少，但旁边就是公共超充站。

🔋 EV Status (充电情报): Paid 付费 Public Hub 公共站
  • Charger: Tesla V3 + ChargEV (at Public Parking).
  • Note: Public chargers might be busy on weekends.
    主要依靠旁边的公共停车场充电，旺季可能需要排队。
Check Prices & Book ➔ 查看房价 & 预订

4. Sunway Hotel Big Box Iskandar Puteri

Why here: Near Legoland & Tuas Checkpoint. A quiet EV hub with plenty of food options. 推荐理由： 靠近 Legoland 和大士关口。商场就在楼下，吃喝玩乐一站式。

🔋 EV Status (充电情报): Paid 付费 Many Slots 位多
  • Charger: Tesla Superchargers + Gentari DC.
  • Note: Usually easy to get a spot.
    通常位子很空，适合作为入境后的第一站补给。
Check Prices & Book ➔ 查看房价 & 预订

5. Impiana Hotel Senai Senai Airport

Why here: Closest hotel to Senai Airport. Good for business trips. 推荐理由： 商务出差首选，离机场最近的星级酒店。

🔋 EV Status (充电情报): Very Limited 极少
  • Charger: Wall socket / Slow AC only.
  • Advice: Better to charge at nearby Petronas/Caltex before checking in.
    建议在附近的油站快充站充好后再入住，比较安心。
