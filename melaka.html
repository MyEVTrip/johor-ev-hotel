Stopover Guide: Best Hotels with EV Charging
新加坡/JB 北上吉隆坡的最佳中转站

Johor (JB) | Melaka | Kuala Lumpur (KL)

1. DoubleTree by Hilton Melaka

马六甲希尔顿逸林酒店 (Hatten City) 💎 Luxury⚡ Wallbox Charging
🔋 Charging Info (充电情报): Located at parking level. 2 units of AC chargers available for guests. 位于停车场层，配有住客专用的交流电 (AC) 充电桩。
🍽️ Eat & Play (吃喝玩乐): Next to Elements Mall. Great view of the Straits of Melaka. Famous for their cookie welcome! 海景房视野超棒。就在 Elements Mall 隔壁。
Agoda Trip.com

2. Hatten Hotel Melaka

惠胜酒店 (City Centre) 📍 Best Location⚡ ChargEV
🔋 Charging Info (充电情报): ChargEV station located at the premier car park entrance. 在停车场入口处设有 ChargEV 充电桩，位置非常显眼。
🍽️ Eat & Play (吃喝玩乐): Walking distance to Dataran Pahlawan & Mahkota Parade. Very close to historical sites. 步行可达英雄广场和各大商场。去红屋、鸡场街都很近。
Agoda Trip.com

3. Courtyard by Marriott Melaka

万怡酒店 (New & Modern) ✨ New Hotel⚡ Porsche Destination
🔋 Charging Info (充电情报): Equipped with Porsche Destination Charging (High power AC). 配备保时捷目的地充电桩（高速 AC），新酒店设备完善。
🍽️ Eat & Play (吃喝玩乐): Near St. Peter's Church. A bit quieter but still very accessible to Jonker Street by Grab. 环境较清幽，设施很新。叫车去鸡场街只需几分钟。
Agoda Trip.com

4. The Pines Melaka

松树酒店 (Riverside) 🌊 River View⚡ EV Bays
🔋 Charging Info (充电情报): Dedicated EV charging bays available in the multi-level parking. 多层停车场内设有专用的电动车充电位。
🍽️ Eat & Play (吃喝玩乐): Spacious rooms suitable for families. Nice view of the Melaka River. 房间很大，适合家庭出游。可以看到马六甲河景。
Agoda Trip.com

5. Ames Hotel

埃姆斯酒店 (Ayer Keroh) 🛣️ Near Toll⚡ Tesla/AC Charging
🔋 Charging Info (充电情报): Strategic stopover near Ayer Keroh Toll. Has AC Chargers & access to nearby Superchargers. 位于 Ayer Keroh 收费站旁，不用塞车进古城。有充电设施，补电休息首选。
🍽️ Eat & Play (吃喝玩乐): Luxury boutique style. Near Melaka Zoo and Wonderland. 豪华精品风格。靠近马六甲动物园和水上乐园，适合亲子游。
Agoda Trip.com
