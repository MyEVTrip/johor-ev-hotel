1. G Hotel Gurney

G 酒店 (Gurney Drive) 📍 Gurney Plaza⚡ Tesla Destination
🔋 Charging Info (充电情报): Tesla Destination Chargers available for guests. 特斯拉车主首选。非特斯拉可去隔壁 Gurney Plaza 地下层充电。
🍜 Eat & Play (吃喝玩乐): Right next to Gurney Drive Hawker Centre. Shopping at Gurney Plaza. 出门就是关仔角夜市和商场。

2. Angsana Teluk Bahang

悦椿度假村 (Beach) 🌊 Seaview⚡ DC Fast Charger
🔋 Charging Info (充电情报): 24kW DC Fast Charger available. 全槟城少有的自带 **DC 快充** 的度假酒店。喝个下午茶也能补电。
🎡 Eat & Play (吃喝玩乐): Near Escape Theme Park & Entopia Butterfly Farm. 靠近 Escape 乐园和蝴蝶园。远离市区，适合纯度假。

3. Hard Rock Hotel Penang

槟城硬石酒店 (Batu Ferringhi) 🎸 Family Fun⚡ Public Nearby
🔋 Charging Info (充电情报): No onsite charger yet, but standard 3-pin available upon request. 酒店内暂无快充，但可询问借用插座。建议在市区充好再来。
🏊 Eat & Play (吃喝玩乐): Huge pool with slides. Night market right outside. 超大泳池，孩子最爱。出门就是夜市。

4. The Prestige Hotel

威望酒店 (Georgetown) 🏛️ Heritage⚡ ChargEV Nearby
🔋 Charging Info (充电情报): Walking distance to public ChargEV stations in Georgetown. 位于古迹区。步行可达附近的公共充电站。
📸 Eat & Play (吃喝玩乐): Most instagrammable hotel. Walk to Bank Street & Cafe hopping. 网红打卡必住。像《布达佩斯大饭店》。走路去银行街拍照。

5. Amari SPICE Penang

阿玛瑞 (Bayan Lepas) ✈️ Airport⚡ Tesla Supercharger
🔋 Charging Info (充电情报): Tesla Supercharger located at SPICE Arena basement. 就在 SPICE Arena 旁边，底下就有特斯拉超充站！商务首选。
🛍️ Eat & Play (吃喝玩乐): Connected to SPICE Convention Centre. 10min to Airport. 直通会展中心。离机场很近。
