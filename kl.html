Top 5 Luxury Hotels with EV Charging & Food Guide
吉隆坡 5 大豪华充电酒店推荐 + 周边吃喝攻略

Johor (JB) | Kuala Lumpur (KL)

1. EQ Kuala Lumpur

EQ 酒店 (KLCC Area) 📍 Golden Triangle⚡ Tesla Destination
🔋 Charging Info (充电情报): Tesla Destination Chargers & Universal AC available. Basement parking reserved for EVs. 设有特斯拉目的地充电桩和通用 AC 桩。地下停车场有 EV 专属车位。
🍽️ Eat & Play (吃喝玩乐): Walking distance to Pavilion Mall & KLCC. Sky51 Rooftop Bar is a must-visit. 步行可达 Pavilion 和 KLCC。必去 Sky51 楼顶酒吧看双峰塔夜景。

2. Mandarin Oriental

文华东方酒店 (KLCC) 📍 Next to Towers⚡ Porsche Charging
🔋 Charging Info (充电情报): Porsche Destination Charging (High speed AC) available. Valet parking assistance. 拥有保时捷高速 AC 充电桩。提供代客泊车充电服务。
🍽️ Eat & Play (吃喝玩乐): Direct access to Suria KLCC & Park. Best Dim Sum at Lai Po Heen. 直通 KLCC 商场和公园。推荐丽宝轩 (Lai Po Heen) 的点心。
Agoda Check Rates Trip.com Points & Deals

3. Le Méridien Kuala Lumpur

吉隆坡艾美酒店 (KL Sentral) 📍 Transport Hub⚡ Shell Recharge
🔋 Charging Info (充电情报): Shell Recharge & ChargEV available in the shared parking complex. 位于交通枢纽，停车场内有 Shell Recharge 和 ChargEV 多种选择。
🍽️ Eat & Play (吃喝玩乐): Next to NU Sentral Mall. 5min walk to Little India (Brickfields) for banana leaf rice. 连接 NU Sentral 商场。步行5分钟到小印度 (Brickfields) 吃香蕉叶饭。
Agoda Check Rates Trip.com Points & Deals

4. Grand Hyatt Kuala Lumpur

君悦酒店 (City Centre) 📍 Near Pavilion⚡ Guest Only
🔋 Charging Info (充电情报): Dedicated EV parking bays for guests. Please request with concierge upon arrival. 住客专用充电位。抵达时请咨询礼宾部安排。
🍽️ Eat & Play (吃喝玩乐): Covered walkway to Pavilion Mall. Near Jalan Alor Food Street (Night Market). 有冷气走道直通 Pavilion 商场。靠近阿罗街 (Jalan Alor) 夜市吃宵夜。

5. Pavilion Hotel Managed by Banyan Tree

柏威年酒店 (Bukit Bintang) 🛍️ Shopping Heaven⚡ Supercharger Hub
🔋 Charging Info (充电情报): Direct access to Pavilion Mall Basement. Largest hub with Tesla Superchargers (V3) & Shell Recharge. 楼下就是吉隆坡最大的充电中心 (Tesla V3 超充 + Shell)，充电最方便的酒店。
🍽️ Eat & Play (吃喝玩乐): Located on top of Pavilion KL. Luxury shopping (Hermès, LV) and endless food options. 位于柏威年商场楼上。名牌购物、美食天堂，去哪里都方便。
