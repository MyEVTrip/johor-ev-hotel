1. The Banjaran Hotsprings Retreat

万雅岚温泉度假村 (Luxury) 💎 Ultra Luxury⚡ BMW/Tesla
🔋 Charging Info (充电情报): BMW Wallbox (7kW) available for guests. Call ahead to reserve. 全马最顶级的温泉度假村。住客专用慢充桩。适合庆祝纪念日。
🍷 Eat & Play (吃喝玩乐): Jeff's Cellar (Cave Bar). Natural Geothermal Hot Springs. 必去 Jeff's Cellar 溶洞酒吧。享受天然地热温泉。

2. The Haven All Suite Resort

曦云轩度假酒店 (Nature) 🌳 Limestone View⚡ ChargeSini
🔋 Charging Info (充电情报): ChargeSini (22kW AC) station located at visitor parking. 被亿万年石灰岩山环绕。停车场有 ChargeSini 扫码充电，非常方便。
🏊 Eat & Play (吃喝玩乐): Iconic seahorse-shaped pool. Near Lost World of Tambun. 标志性的海马形泳池。靠近 Tambun 迷失乐园。

3. Weil Hotel

唯裕酒店 (City Centre) 🛍️ Connected to Mall⚡ Mall Charging
🔋 Charging Info (充电情报): Directly connected to Ipoh Parade. Chargers available at Mall Basement. 直通 Ipoh Parade 商场。可以把车停在商场地下层充电，然后上楼入住。
🍽️ Eat & Play (吃喝玩乐): Rooftop infinity pool with city view. Walk to famous Dim Sum street. 顶楼无边际泳池。走路可到点心街 (富山/明阁)。

4. M Roof Hotel & Residences

M Roof 酒店 (Modern) 🏢 Modern Design⚡ ChargeSini
🔋 Charging Info (充电情报): ChargeSini (22kW AC) stations located right at the entrance parking. 酒店门口就有 ChargeSini 充电桩，非常方便。现代工业风设计。
🏟️ Eat & Play (吃喝玩乐): Near Perak Stadium. Many hipster cafes nearby. 靠近体育馆。附近有很多网红咖啡馆。
